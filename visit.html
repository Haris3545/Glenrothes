Find Us

Rothes, Moray, Speyside

The distillery sits on the edge of Rothes, on the banks of the Burn of Rothes, roughly ten miles south of Elgin and a comfortable drive from Aberdeen, Inverness, or the rest of the Speyside whisky trail.
