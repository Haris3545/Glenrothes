How It's Made

From spring water to sealed cask

01

Water

Soft spring water is drawn from the hills above Rothes and filtered slowly through peat and rock.

02

Malt & Ferment

Malted barley is mashed and fermented over several days to develop the fruit-forward character we ferment for.

03

Distil

A double distillation through copper pot stills, shaped and run to favour a clean, elegant new-make spirit.

04

Mature

Filled almost exclusively into sherry-seasoned oak and left in earthen-floored warehouses for years, sometimes decades.
